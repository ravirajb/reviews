# The Dark Knight (2008)

## Overview
- **Director:** Christopher Nolan
- **Stars:** Christian Bale, Heath Ledger, Aaron Eckhart
- **Rating:** 9.0/10
- **Duration:** 2h 32m
- **Genre:** Action, Crime, Drama

## Plot Summary
When the menace known as the Joker wreaks havoc and chaos on the people of Gotham, Batman must accept one of the greatest psychological and physical tests of his ability to fight injustice.

## Review

### Strengths
- Heath Ledger's iconic performance as the Joker
- Complex moral dilemmas and philosophical questions
- Tight, engaging storytelling with perfect pacing
- Stunning visual effects and cinematography
- A perfect blend of action and drama

### Weaknesses
- Some viewers might find the dark tone overwhelming
- The complex plot might be confusing for some

### Key Takeaways
- The duality of human nature
- The concept of chaos vs. order
- The burden of responsibility
- The theme of sacrifice

### Notable Quotes
- "Why so serious?"
- "It's not who I am underneath, but what I do that defines me."

## Final Verdict
A groundbreaking superhero film that transcends the genre. The Dark Knight is a masterclass in filmmaking that combines thrilling action with profound philosophical themes.

**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5)
