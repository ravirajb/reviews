# The Good, the Bad and the Ugly (1966)

## Overview
- **Director:** Sergio Leone
- **Stars:** Clint Eastwood, Lee Van Cleef, Eli Wallach
- **Rating:** 8.9/10
- **Duration:** 2h 58m
- **Genre:** Western

## Plot Summary
A bounty hunting scam joins two men in an uneasy alliance against a third in a race to find a fortune in gold buried in a remote cemetery.

## Review

### Strengths
- Iconic performances and character development
- Ennio Morricone's legendary score
- Cinematic excellence in every frame
- Perfect pacing and tension building
- Timeless themes of greed and morality

### Weaknesses
- Some viewers might find the violence graphic
- The slow build might test patience

### Key Takeaways
- The complexities of morality in extreme circumstances
- The theme of survival and self-interest
- The power of music in storytelling
- The importance of timing and strategy

### Notable Quotes
- "When you have to shoot, shoot, don't talk."
- "If you've got to ask, you'll never know."

## Final Verdict
A masterpiece of the Western genre that continues to influence filmmakers and audiences alike. The Good, the Bad and the Ugly is a cinematic triumph that combines style, substance, and unforgettable performances.

**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5)
