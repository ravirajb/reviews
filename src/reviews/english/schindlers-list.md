# Schindler's List (1993)

## Overview
- **Director:** Steven Spielberg
- **Stars:** Liam Neeson, Ben Kingsley, Ralph Fiennes
- **Rating:** 8.9/10
- **Duration:** 3h 15m
- **Genre:** Biography, Drama, History

## Plot Summary
In German-occupied Poland during World War II, industrialist Oskar Schindler gradually becomes concerned for his Jewish workforce after witnessing their persecution by the Nazis.

## Review

### Strengths
- Powerful and emotionally resonant storytelling
- Outstanding performances by the entire cast
- Cinematic excellence in every aspect
- Important historical significance
- Thought-provoking exploration of morality

### Weaknesses
- The subject matter is extremely heavy and disturbing
- The length might be challenging for some viewers

### Key Takeaways
- The power of individual action in the face of evil
- The complexities of morality in extreme circumstances
- The importance of remembering history
- The theme of redemption through action

### Notable Quotes
- "The list is an absolute good. The list is life."
- "I could've got more. I could've got more..."

## Final Verdict
A monumental achievement in cinema that serves as both a powerful historical document and a profound exploration of human nature. Schindler's List is a necessary and unforgettable film that demands to be seen and remembered.

**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5)
