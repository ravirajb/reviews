# 12 Angry Men (1957)

## Overview
- **Director:** Sidney Lumet
- **Stars:** Henry Fonda, Lee J. Cobb, Martin Balsam
- **Rating:** 8.9/10
- **Duration:** 1h 36m
- **Genre:** Drama, Crime

## Plot Summary
A jury of 12 men is sent to deliberate the fate of a young man accused of murdering his father. As the evidence points to guilt, one juror raises doubts about the case, leading to a tense and revealing debate.

## Review

### Strengths
- Outstanding ensemble cast performances
- Tight, intelligent script with no wasted moments
- Powerful exploration of human nature and prejudice
- Masterful use of a single setting
- Timeless themes that remain relevant

### Weaknesses
- The single-setting might be challenging for some viewers
- The slow build might test patience

### Key Takeaways
- The importance of critical thinking and reasonable doubt
- The impact of personal biases on decision-making
- The power of one voice to challenge the majority
- The complexities of justice and morality

### Notable Quotes
- "We're talking about somebody's life here. We can't decide in five minutes."
- "I don't want to change your mind. I just want to talk."

## Final Verdict
A masterclass in filmmaking that demonstrates the power of dialogue and character development. 12 Angry Men remains a powerful exploration of justice, morality, and human nature that continues to resonate with audiences today.

**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5/5)
